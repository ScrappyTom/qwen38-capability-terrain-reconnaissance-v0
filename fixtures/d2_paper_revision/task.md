# Revise an existing paper from locked evidence

Revise `paper.md` using the two locked source records
`evidence_update.md` and `editorial_memo.md`.

The revision must:

- update the Abstract, Results, and Discussion to agree with the current
  design record, primary analysis, sensor-completeness audit, and editorial
  memo;
- use association language appropriate to the study design rather than making
  a causal claim;
- report the current primary group rates, adjusted difference, and confidence
  interval in both the Abstract and Results, and report the current p value in
  Results;
- report the group-specific sensor-dropout rates and explain the stated
  direction of possible bias from the complete-case analysis without
  imputation;
- characterize the ward-level subgroup analysis according to the locked
  records without presenting it as confirmatory;
- bind design statements to `[D1]`, primary-result statements to `[D2]`, and
  missingness or subgroup statements to `[D3]`; and
- add no source, statistic, or recommendation not supported by the locked
  records.

Preserve the title and the complete Introduction, Methods, and References
sections of `paper.md` verbatim. Keep the existing section headings in their
existing order. Do not change `evidence_update.md` or `editorial_memo.md`.
Only `paper.md` should change.

The declared `visible` check covers candidate file identity, locked-source and
protected-section preservation, section structure, obsolete literal markers,
and citation syntax. External grading evaluates the revised evidence claims
and cross-section consistency. Submit when you choose to finish.
