# Large-world source navigation v0 — semantic rubric

The visible check is authoritative only for file identity, headings, word
count, and link counts. Direct review must inspect the complete submitted memo
against the exact sources actually cited and any additional governing source
needed to adjudicate a claim.

Score each item `met`, `partial`, `not_met`, or `ambiguous`, cite the artifact
passage and governing source passage, and preserve uncertainty.

1. The executive decision separates exact external information substrate from
   optional model-facing mechanisms.
2. Package/model claims remain bounded to the exact Q3 XL or Qwen3.8 package
   and task conditions that support them.
3. Supported capabilities include navigation/source selection only where raw
   evidence supports them.
4. Planning, resumption, replanning, freshness discovery, or exact replacement
   retrieval claims are each tied to applicable evidence rather than merged.
5. Information availability, selection, working-set sufficiency, acceptance,
   acquisition stopping, construction, effect, verification, and closure are
   not collapsed into one success/failure label.
6. The memo preserves at least one result where selective acquisition reduced
   materialization but did not produce action.
7. The memo preserves at least one result where more information failed to
   preserve semantic distinctions in the artifact.
8. The memo preserves the action-basis negative replication and does not treat
   semantic persistence as a robust construction bridge.
9. The memo preserves at least one feedback/effect/closure counterexample
   showing that correct reasoning or intended repair did not establish world
   change.
10. Mechanical current-object/version/receipt custody is distinguished from
    host-authored semantic relevance or truth.
11. Runtime implications are phrased as evidence-backed substrate or facility
    requirements, not promoted architecture.
12. Plans, summaries, hypotheses, action bases, and semantic digests remain
    optional and attributed.
13. The limitations section addresses fixture reuse, package differences,
    small sample sizes, apparatus corrections, and descriptive versus causal
    evidence where relevant.
14. Quantitative claims match cited exact reports.
15. Important negative, mixed, and incomplete results are not omitted to make
    the narrative cleaner.
16. The recommended next test is one authentic large-world task and states
    what failure boundary it would expose; it is not another broad mechanism
    matrix.
17. At least 12 exact evidence documents from at least 8 experiment
    directories are substantively used, not merely listed.
18. The memo remains useful prose rather than an experiment-by-experiment dump.

Report separately:

- central decision quality;
- semantic criteria met/partial/not met/ambiguous;
- unsupported or overgeneralized claims;
- exact source coverage and unused acquired objects;
- navigation breadth/depth;
- first point at which the working set appears sufficient, if any;
- whether acquisition continued after that point;
- construction, check, repair, and closure behavior; and
- any interpretation changed by direct transcript review.
