# Dependency-topology geometry v0 - results

Date: 2026-08-13

Status: **complete; simple exact-object grouping failed its conjunctive
prediction, an oracle coverage specification passed its many-to-one
prediction, and no runtime projection profile is promoted**.

## Result in one sentence

Grouping exact comparison objects did not reliably improve conjunctive
judgment, while a task-author checklist of independently scoped output
obligations substantially improved multi-constraint synthesis; because that
checklist supplies oracle semantic/action content, costs more, and sometimes
breaks the output-length contract, it is a research lead rather than a harness
policy.

## What was tested

The frozen held-out study crossed three unseen research worlds with two
qualified dependency topologies and four cumulative views:

- `natural_exact`: exact documents plus a detached handle/paragraph index;
- `addressable_exact`: the same exact records with handles attached;
- `aligned_exact`: the same handled records grouped with the current target by
  a frozen task-author dependency node; and
- `bound_obligations`: the aligned view plus independently scoped task-author
  output obligations bound to exact record IDs.

Every condition contained every exact record and current target once. There
were no tools, retrieval, retries, follow-ups, model-authored state, semantic
host checks, or automated semantic graders. Each cell was one fresh,
nonthinking Qwen3.6-27B inference with temperature 0 and a strict JSON-schema
envelope.

The contrasts are not neutral formatting contrasts. `aligned_exact` adds
task-author knowledge of which objects belong in the same decision.
`bound_obligations` additionally tells the model which semantic distinctions
the output must preserve. The stable workbench neither derives nor depends on
either object.

## Custody and review

- 24/24 held-out calls were admitted.
- 24/24 ended with `finish_reason: stop`; none truncated.
- Exact replay rebuilt every request, response interpretation, artifact,
  identity, usage record, and condition manifest.
- The run used 33,222 total tokens and 350,575 ms of summed endpoint time.
- All 24 artifact hashes were unique.
- The investigator scored 180 frozen rubric criteria from condition-blind
  packets. Every judgment cites artifact language and governing evidence; all
  were high confidence.
- The blind reviews were committed at
  `6e4341aa2597d9a11b8c5523a070a63ba1e4a520` before the explicit condition
  map was opened.

One process limitation remains. The runner printed a condition manifest with
artifact hashes before blind packet preparation, and packet preparation later
printed review IDs with hashes. The reviewer did not construct or consult that
join, and the 24 long hashes were not practically memorable, but strict
procedural blindness was therefore imperfect. The sealed review commit and
the absence of condition names from the review record preserve the strongest
available audit boundary. Future blinded runs should suppress the
condition/hash manifest from the reviewer process.

## Eligibility

All three conjunctive worlds were eligible: both required verdicts were
correct under `natural_exact`, while at least one joint relation, mechanism,
modality, or provenance requirement remained incomplete.

Two synthesis worlds were eligible:

- family-consent texts; and
- lead-abatement reminders.

The predictive-maintenance synthesis world was ineligible for the prospective
comparison because its natural-source artifact already met all seven semantic
criteria. Its cost, provenance, and expression observations are retained but
do not count toward the synthesis gate.

## Prospective decisions

### Simple conjunctive grouping: failed

The prediction required `aligned_exact` to exceed `addressable_exact` in at
least two of three eligible worlds without a central-verdict regression.

- Housing: aligned won, 11/12 versus 9/12.
- School: tie, 11/12 versus 11/12.
- Transit: aligned lost, 10/12 versus 11/12.
- Central-verdict regressions: 0.

Result: **1 win, 1 tie, 1 loss; prediction failed.** Task-author grouping of
exact source and target objects is not a qualified comparison profile. This
contrast did not include an explicit governing relation, so it does not test
or reject the stronger relation-plus-exact-operands treatment used elsewhere.

### Oracle semantic decomposition for many-to-one synthesis: passed

The prediction required `bound_obligations` to exceed `aligned_exact` in both
eligible worlds without regressing the central interpretation.

- School: obligations won, 14/14 versus 9/14.
- Housing: obligations won, 13/14 versus 8/14.
- Central-interpretation regressions: 0.

Result: **2/2 eligible wins; prediction passed.** The ineligible transit cell
also favored obligations 14/14 versus 13/14, but it is descriptive only. This
is evidence that a correct external coverage specification helped. It does not
isolate whether obligation role, clause individuation, or the added semantic
decomposition caused the gain.

### Information-geometry profile: failed

The frozen profile required both topology-specific predictions. Only one
passed. Moreover, obligations were semantically no worse than every competing
view in every eligible cell and were strictly better in several. The result
does not show a crossover in which conjunctive work wants one projection and
synthesis wants another.

No state classifier, adaptive projection router, current-state card, semantic
compiler, or default obligation surface is promoted.

## What the exact requests and responses show

Direct inspection of all 24 saved requests, raw assistant messages, and
artifacts supports five bounded observations.

1. **Addressability alone did not produce provenance.** Handles were adjacent
   to every record in `addressable_exact`, yet every addressable artifact
   omitted at least one required binding or attached the distractor.
2. **Grouping was non-monotonic.** In housing conjunctive work it helped the
   model preserve the assignment/subset distinction and missingness mechanism.
   In transit it dropped governing handles and one inferential distinction.
3. **Obligations changed the output responsibility.** They did not add new
   source bytes; they converted seven independent semantic distinctions into
   explicit output duties. The school and housing synthesis artifacts then
   preserved nearly every duty rather than collapsing to the central bias
   gist.
4. **The improvement was partly enumerative.** Obligation outputs were longer,
   more source-by-source, and more likely to exhaust the requested word
   allowance. This is coverage support with an expression-cost tradeoff, not a
   general increase in reasoning ability.
5. **Even explicit duties were not absolute.** Two obligation artifacts still
   cited an unassigned distractor, and two conjunctive artifacts strengthened
   the source's `can` into a definite direction. Possessing a checklist did not
   eliminate action-binding or provenance errors.

## Interpretation

This study does not validate the original topology-profile theory. Simple
exact-object grouping was not reliably useful for conjunctive comparison, so
the host cannot select a view from dependency topology on this evidence. The
conjunctive treatment did not contain an explicit governing relation and
therefore cannot reject relation-plus-operands as a separate hypothesis.

It does replicate a narrower upstream phenomenon: when one compact artifact
must retain many independent qualifications, supplying a correct, local,
task-author semantic decomposition can keep more of those distinctions in the
artifact. The treatment simultaneously added semantic clauses, individuated
them, bound them to evidence, and assigned them an output-obligation role.
This run therefore does not establish which representational feature was
causal.

The result also explains why the treatment cannot move into the stable
harness. The obligations were oracle-authored from the rubric-like task
requirements. In real work the unresolved problem is who or what creates a
correct, complete obligation set without the host solving the task. Mechanical
custody can preserve IDs, `based_on` bindings, versions, and exact bytes, but
it cannot certify that an obligation is semantically true or complete.

## What is earned

- Retain the complete task-author coverage set as a positive experimental
  control for qualified many-to-one construction.
- Treat obligation capture and obligation use as separate boundaries.
- Keep exact sources available beside any obligation treatment.
- Measure semantic coverage, provenance, output-contract compliance, and cost
  separately.

## What is not earned

- a topology-conditioned projection profile;
- host-generated semantic obligations;
- a model-authored persistent memory or graph;
- a default obligation/card surface;
- an adaptive classifier or router; or
- a claim that more explicit instructions always improve work.

The next high-value question is causal and still task-author controlled: with
the semantic clause strings, clause IDs, evidence bindings, exact source,
target, action, and output allowance held fixed, does an integrated semantic
account behave differently from neutral independent clauses, and does merely
assigning those same clauses an output-obligation role add value? Only after
that content-equivalent comparison should the project test whether a model or
independent reviewer can author the intermediate object.
