# Action-role x binding factorial v0 - results

## Result

The comparison was mechanically clean and eligible, but neither action-role
labeling nor repeated exact scope binding met its frozen lead rule.

All 24 calls were admitted and replayed exactly. Every condition preserved the
U005 no-op byte-for-byte. The reference-unbound control remained below pass on
four of five revision units and satisfied 10/22 action-item groups, so the
experiment instantiated the intended incomplete-construction regime.

| Condition | Pass / partial / fail | Items | Revision directions | Pair guards | Total tokens |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `reference_unbound` | 2 / 3 / 1 | 10/22 | 4/5 | 0/3 | 7,024 |
| `reference_bound` | 2 / 3 / 1 | 9/22 | 4/5 | 0/3 | 7,901 |
| `obligation_unbound` | 3 / 3 / 0 | 13/22 | 5/5 | 1/3 | 7,008 |
| `obligation_bound` | 3 / 2 / 1 | 11/22 | 4/5 | 1/3 | 7,916 |

No condition reached the complete-method requirement of 6/6 units, 22/22
items, all three pair guards, and the exact no-op.

## What changed

Changing `reference_content` to `required_final_unit_content` without repeated
scope improved only U006: the model replaced an unsupported PM2.5 claim with
the exact telemetry and unmeasured-outcome boundary. That changed one unit
from fail to pass and added three items. U001 remained partial, U003 and U004
still collapsed their four-item missingness chains to short directional gists,
and U002 and U005 were already complete. The frozen lead required at least two
improved grades and four additional items, so this arm did not qualify.

With repeated scope present, the obligation role again repaired U006, but it
also changed U001 from a partial randomized-design repair to the exact original
and incorrect observational claim. The net item gain was two, with one grade
improvement, one grade regression, one central-direction gain, and one central-
direction loss. That contrast is disqualified by regression.

Repeated binding did not improve a unit grade under either role:

| Contrast | Grade gains | Grade losses | Item delta | Exact same outputs | Token delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| obligation vs reference, unbound | 1 | 0 | +3 | 2/6 | -16 (-0.23%) |
| obligation vs reference, bound | 1 | 1 | +2 | 3/6 | +15 (+0.19%) |
| bound vs unbound, reference | 0 | 0 | -1 | 4/6 | +877 (+12.49%) |
| bound vs unbound, obligation | 0 | 1 | -2 | 4/6 | +908 (+12.96%) |

The bound conditions repeated only already-visible artifact, version, binding,
and evidence identities. They added no world fact. In this fixture they added
roughly one eighth more tokens, gained no grade, and under the obligation role
lost the randomized correction.

## Direct trajectory findings

The same exact item lines were present in all four arms. Their use was sharply
unit-dependent:

- U002 was complete in all four conditions.
- U005 was an exact no-op in all four conditions.
- U003 and U004 remained gist-only in all four conditions. None retained a
  complete coverage, scale, missingness-location, mean-shift, modality, and
  negative-contrast chain.
- U006 changed only with the declared obligation role; binding did not matter.
- U001 did not retain explicit causal support in any condition. Repeated scope
  reduced one reference-arm item and, combined with the obligation role,
  produced an exact incorrect no-op.

These are literal output patterns, not claims about attention, memory slots,
or internal model psychology.

## Interpretation

The result weakens the proposed forensic explanation of the earlier 20/22 and
21/22 semantic-frame results. Those gains cannot be attributed simply to:

1. calling the same ID-bearing lines required final-output content;
2. repeating exact artifact/evidence scope beside them; or
3. combining those two fields in this controlled representation.

The obligation label was not inert: it reliably changed U006 and changed other
wording. But it was selective and non-monotonic rather than a generally useful
construction constraint. Repeated exact identity was also not neutral in cost
or behavior, despite adding no semantic fact.

This experiment does not prove that every action-facing obligation rendering
is useless. The older successful frame used a different complete wrapper,
placement, and fixture. It does show that progressively reconstructing that
frame one guessed feature at a time has not recovered its benefit. Tuning this
fixture or adding another wrapper would now be reactive.

## Decision

Promote nothing from this factorial. Do not add obligation fields, repeated
scope blocks, cards, a compiler, persistent semantic state, or a semantic host
gate to the stable workbench.

Preserve the earlier semantic-frame effect as a bounded empirical result whose
active ingredient is unresolved. The next project decision should be a zoomed-
out choice among genuinely different intervention families, not another small
format mutation derived from these six outputs.
