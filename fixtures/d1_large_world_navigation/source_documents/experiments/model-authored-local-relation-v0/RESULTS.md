# Model-authored local relation v0 — results

## Outcome

Neither the model-authored relation nor the complete task-author relation
qualified. They failed for importantly different reasons.

- The model producer graded 0 pass / 5 partial / 1 fail and captured 10/22
  frozen relation items. On U001 it made the central comparison incorrectly:
  it said an observational current sentence already reflected randomized
  assignment, a two-sided p-value, and appropriate attribution.
- `direct_local` graded 1 pass / 5 partial / 0 fail with 4/22 action items.
- `model_relation` graded 1 pass / 4 partial / 1 fail with 5/22 items. It added
  one item but regressed U001 from a partial repair to an unchanged failed
  action.
- `oracle_relation` graded 1 pass / 4 partial / 1 fail with 4/22 items. Its
  complete 22-item relationship paragraph added no frozen item over direct,
  regressed U006 to an unsupported no-effect assertion, and therefore failed
  the positive-control gate.

All six relation calls and all eighteen action calls were structurally
admitted. Every condition preserved the exact no-op. Exact replay passed.

The clean conclusion is not simply that the model failed to author useful
state. The task-author paragraph also failed. This run therefore establishes:

> Complete local semantic content expressed as one relationship paragraph is
> not equivalent, on this fresh family, to the separately itemized local
> required-content frame that succeeded in the two earlier fixtures.

That is evidence about the information view, not evidence for a permanent
card, compiler, or semantic host.

## Direct review of what the model actually saw and did

The investigator inspected all six exact Phase-A requests and raw responses,
all six admitted relation artifacts, all eighteen exact Phase-B requests and
raw responses, all eighteen final units and transitions, the endpoint/profile
snapshot, and every frozen item before adjudication.

### Model-authored relation capture

The producer was structurally exact: every relation retained its fixed target
version and exact evidence ID/hash. Semantic capture was much weaker.

- **U001 — fail, 0/3.** The evidence and current unit plainly disagreed:
  evidence said computer-schedule random assignment while the unit said
  observational. The relation instead declared that the unit “accurately
  reflects the randomized assignment design,” correctly reports a two-sided
  result, and appropriately attributes the difference. This was not an
  omission; it was a false comparison of the two exact operands.
- **U002 — partial, 2/4.** It retained the exact adjusted association and the
  noncausal interpretation. It omitted why assignment was nonrandom and
  omitted residual confounding.
- **U003 — partial, 2/4.** It retained the low-score-omission → raised-mean →
  can-exaggerate relation, but dropped both coverage rates and the score-scale
  and nonresponse details.
- **U004 — partial, 1/4.** It retained high-score omission → lower mean, but
  dropped coverage and score details and strengthened `can attenuate` into
  “thereby attenuating.”
- **U005 — partial, 3/4.** It correctly recognized exact record alignment and
  all measured values, but did not retain the task's exact-preservation
  obligation.
- **U006 — partial, 2/3.** It rejected the unsupported 12% result and retained
  the unmeasured/cannot-establish relation, but omitted every field the logs
  did measure.

The producer therefore failed every frozen qualification clause: fewer than
5/6 passing relations, fewer than 20/22 items, one central pair failure, and
material unsupported assertions.

### Direct local action

The direct condition behaved like the familiar gist-first baseline.

- U001 changed `observational` to `randomized` but omitted the assignment
  method, prespecified ITT/two-sided status, and causal implication.
- U002 and U004 were left unchanged.
- U003 corrected attenuation to `can exaggerate` but omitted coverage, the
  missing-score mechanism, and the negative contrast.
- U005 was preserved byte-for-byte.
- U006 removed 12% but returned only “Shuttle texts did not measure riders'
  indoor heat exposure,” omitting both the measured fields and the
  cannot-establish conclusion.

It recovered all six central directions but only one complete unit and 4/22
frozen item groups.

### Model relation → action

The model relation was not inert; on its most inaccurate unit it was harmful.

- U001 received the false “already accurate” relation and resubmitted the
  original observational sentence byte-for-byte. The direct arm at least
  changed it to randomized.
- U002, U003, and U004 were byte-identical to direct despite their relations
  containing correct distinctions absent from the final text.
- U005 was correctly preserved in every condition.
- U006 improved on direct by explicitly calling heat exposure unmeasured, but
  still omitted the recorded fields and explicit inability to establish
  change.

Across all units, 4 of 10 correctly captured relation items had corresponding
action items represented. Three were already present in the U005 no-op. Among
the five units requiring revision, only 1 of 7 correctly captured relation
items survived into a corresponding frozen action item.

This is literal relation-to-action transfer, not a claim about an internal
mental state.

### Complete oracle relation → action

The task-author relation contained all 22 semantic items, used the same
identity/object shape as the model relation, and remained beside the exact
task, evidence, and target. It still did not qualify.

- U001 added randomized and `two-sided`, but omitted computer scheduling,
  prespecified ITT, and bounded causal support.
- U002 added the coordinator selection basis, but omitted explicit nonrandom
  assignment, adjusted status, causal limitation, and residual confounding.
- U003 and U004 were byte-identical to direct: the complete missingness chains
  collapsed to the same short central-polarity sentences.
- U005 remained exact.
- U006 said the texts “did not reduce” heat exposure because logs lacked heat
  measures. That turns absence of measurement into evidence of no effect—the
  exact error the task prohibited.

The oracle affected three outputs, so it was behaviorally active. But it
improved no unit grade, added zero complete frozen item groups, lost one
central direction, and regressed U006 from partial to fail.

## Interpretation

This experiment resolves one ambiguity and exposes another.

First, a model-authored local paragraph is not a trustworthy working object.
It compressed 22 distinctions to 10, misstated the most obvious target/source
conflict, and then caused that false alignment to govern the next action.
Exact evidence remained visible, so evidence custody did not prevent the
lossy transform from anchoring the action.

Second, model authorship is not the whole explanation. The complete oracle
paragraph also failed. The two earlier positive semantic-frame studies did
not merely place correct facts nearby: their so-called prose frame rendered
each required relationship as a separate ID-bearing line. This experiment
placed the same class of complete content into one relationship paragraph.

Across different fresh fixtures, the observed pattern is now:

```text
exact evidence alone                     -> central gist, incomplete action
complete broad semantic prose            -> fewer reads, unreliable action
complete local relationship paragraph    -> central gist, incomplete action
separately itemized local requirements    -> 20/22 and 21/22 action items
```

The final contrast is not a controlled same-fixture representation effect.
Task facts and fixture text also changed, so itemization/segmentation is a
lead, not a causal conclusion. What is ruled out is the simpler claim that a
complete local semantic relationship, merely present beside its operands, is
sufficient.

## Decision

- Do not promote or tune the model-authored relation paragraph.
- Do not promote the oracle paragraph, a bounded context compiler, semantic
  host validation, a verifier role, or a default state surface.
- Do not rerun or restructure this deterministic fixture.
- Retain the experiment as evidence that both semantic capture and literal
  action binding must be measured independently.
- Preserve the earlier itemized local-frame result as the active positive
  lead. If another model-visible test is authorized, it should use a fresh
  fixture and isolate content-equivalent paragraph versus atomic itemization,
  before asking the model to author those items.

The stable `workbench/` and model-server profile remain unchanged.

## Limitations

- Six synthetic units and one deterministic call per cell do not estimate
  run-to-run or task-distribution variance.
- The oracle failed its positive-control gate, so this run cannot estimate a
  clean model-authorship effect.
- The oracle paragraph and earlier itemized frame were tested on different
  fixtures; their contrast combines representation with task facts.
- The relation producer was explicitly prompted to preserve semantic classes;
  its output therefore tests that preparation treatment, not spontaneous
  note-taking.
- The action was one exact unit, not section recomposition or a complete
  research workflow.
