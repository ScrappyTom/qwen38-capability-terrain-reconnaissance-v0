# Task: write the Q3 XL large-world runtime evidence memo

Create a decision-support memo at `Q3XL_LARGE_WORLD_RUNTIME_MEMO.md` for the
next phase of this project.

The exact evidence universe is the frozen external atlas disclosed in the
current work surface. It contains completed experiment result reports, direct
transcript audits, and semantic reviews from this repository at one exact Git
commit. The corpus is intentionally too large to place in active context.

Use the mechanical hierarchy, literal search, and exact reads to determine:

1. which Q3 XL capabilities are supported under the tested conditions;
2. which failures or variable behaviors recur across information selection,
   semantic/detail preservation, construction, effect/check uptake, and
   closure;
3. which runtime substrate properties have direct evidence behind them;
4. which proposed mechanisms remain optional, negative, inconclusive, or
   parked;
5. what important package, fixture, measurement, and causal limits constrain
   the conclusions; and
6. the smallest worthwhile next authentic large-world test.

Required artifact structure:

- `# Q3 XL large-world runtime evidence memo`
- `## Executive decision`
- `## Supported capabilities`
- `## Observed boundaries and recurring errors`
- `## Runtime implications`
- `## Optional or parked mechanisms`
- `## Limits and counterexamples`
- `## Recommended next authentic test`
- `## Evidence index`

Requirements:

- Distinguish model/package observations from general model claims.
- Distinguish information availability, selection, sufficiency judgment,
  acquisition stopping, construction, effect, verification, and closure.
- Preserve important negative and mixed results rather than telling a simple
  progress story.
- Do not call a mechanism supported merely because it reduced reads, tokens,
  or overflow.
- Treat model-authored plans, summaries, action bases, and hypotheses as
  attributed artifacts rather than authoritative world state.
- Use repository-relative Markdown links to exact evidence documents.
- Include at least 12 evidence links spanning at least 8 experiment
  directories.
- State quantitative results only when the exact source supports them.
- Do not claim that the atlas, current-object projection, memory, a controller,
  a commit gate, semantic digests, or any other runtime component is promoted.
- Keep the memo between 1,400 and 2,400 words, excluding the Evidence index.

The visible check validates artifact identity, required structure, link shape,
and word-count bounds. It does not decide whether the memo is semantically
accurate or whether its evidence is sufficient.

Work until the memo is accurate and useful, check it, and submit it. Do not
modify another file.
